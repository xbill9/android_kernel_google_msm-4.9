More of keeping an old Pixel 3a out of a landfill, with Claude working through the radio logs beside me.

My Pixel 3a runs Android 17, LineageOS 24.0, on its original 4.9 kernel. Now it gets mobile data from a Google Fi data-only SIM, IPv4 and IPv6, from a fresh install with nothing set by hand.

What it took:

- 🟢 Mobile data on by default. With it off, the phone dropped off LTE seconds after attaching.
- 🟢 A Google Fi APN for 310260.
- 🟢 VoLTE marked available for Fi in Google's carrier settings, so Android leaves IMS on in the modem. On a stock phone that answer comes from the Google Fi app.
- 🟢 A close_range() backport to the 4.9 kernel, so Android 17 can start clatd for IPv4.
